# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# CampusQR

Version: 1.0

Status: Planning

Last Updated: July 2026

---

# 1. Introduction

CampusQR is a QR-powered digital campus companion that provides visitors, students, faculty, and staff with quick access to navigation, campus information, and essential services through a single QR code scan.

Instead of searching through an entire city map or asking for directions, users receive a focused digital experience specifically designed for the campus they are visiting.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Large campuses can be difficult to navigate, especially for first-time visitors.

Users often struggle to locate:

- Academic departments
- Classrooms
- Laboratories
- Administrative offices
- Library
- Cafeteria
- Hostel
- Parking
- Emergency services

Current solutions rely on physical signboards or general-purpose navigation applications that are not optimized for campus-specific information.

---

# 3. Proposed Solution

CampusQR provides a location-aware digital campus experience.

After scanning a QR code placed at the campus entrance or designated locations, users gain access to:

- Interactive campus map
- Building directory
- Navigation
- Campus information
- Emergency contacts
- Important facilities

---

# 4. Vision

To transform physical campuses into interactive digital environments where users can access navigation, information, and essential services through a single QR code.

---

# 5. Objectives

## Primary Objectives

- Improve campus navigation
- Reduce visitor confusion
- Provide centralized campus information
- Improve overall visitor experience

## Secondary Objectives

- Reduce dependency on physical maps
- Support digital transformation initiatives
- Build a scalable platform for future expansion

---

# 6. Target Users

## Visitors

Need quick navigation and campus information.

## Students

Need fast access to buildings and facilities.

## Faculty

Need easy access to campus information.

## Administrative Staff

Need to manage campus information.

---

# 7. MVP Scope

Version 1 will include:

- QR Code Scanning
- Home Dashboard
- Interactive Campus Map
- Search Buildings
- Building Details
- Navigation
- Emergency Contacts

---

# 8. Future Scope

The following features are intentionally excluded from Version 1.

- Indoor Navigation
- 3D Campus
- AI Assistant
- Event Management
- Notifications
- Analytics Dashboard
- Computer Vision
- Smart City Support
- Multi-Campus Support

---

# 9. Functional Requirements

The system shall allow users to:

- Scan QR codes
- View campus map
- Search campus locations
- View building information
- Navigate to selected locations
- Access emergency contacts

The system shall allow administrators to:

- Add buildings
- Update information
- Generate QR codes
- Manage campus data

---

# 10. Non-Functional Requirements

- Mobile Friendly
- Secure
- Fast Response Time
- Easy to Maintain
- Scalable
- Reliable

---

# 11. Success Metrics

The MVP is considered successful if users can:

- Scan a QR code
- Find a destination
- Navigate successfully
- Access campus information

---

# 12. Assumptions

- QR codes are available at designated campus locations.
- Internet connectivity is available.
- Campus information remains updated.
- Users have smartphones.

---

# 13. Constraints

- Version 1 focuses only on a single campus.
- Outdoor navigation only.
- No offline support in the initial release.
- No AI-based features in Version 1.

---

# 14. Risks

- Outdated campus information
- Poor QR placement
- Changes in campus layout
- Internet connectivity issues

---

# 15. Future Vision

CampusQR is designed as the first implementation of a larger platform capable of supporting:

- Universities
- Hospitals
- Shopping Malls
- Airports
- Tourist Attractions
- Smart Cities

The long-term goal is to create a QR-powered digital experience platform adaptable to any physical location.
